# Manual-nudge catalogue: F1 optimal-lap pipeline (as of 2026-10-19)

## Sources and coverage
- **Claude transcripts.** 17 sessions in `~/.claude/projects/D--f1-hot-lap-OPTIMAL-LAP-SCRIPTS/` (a2f4e2d2 skipped). They cover 2026-07-19 to 2026-10-19. 369 user messages were extracted with `scratchpad/extract.py`, and the output is in `scratchpad/user_msgs.txt`.
- **Codex sessions.** There are 31 F1 sessions in `~/.codex/sessions`. Most of them are imports of the Claude sessions above, or approval-assessor transcripts. Three are unique:
  - `01a0d458`: Baku, done by GPT.
  - `01a06d1b`: the emergency Monza run, which has no physics nudges.
  - `01a090e8-32e8`: the Zandvoort session, imported from a Claude session that no longer exists.
- **Memory only.** The Claude transcripts for Monaco (75d3cc35), Austria (3760945c), Silverstone (ee91a571), Catalunya, Canada and Spa are not on disk. Their nudges below come from the memory notes, so the round counts for them are approximate.
- **Sandbox notes.** The per-track notes in `OPTIMAL LAP SCRIPTS_sandbox/cache/*.md` were also read. They confirm the Singapore, Bahrain, Sepang and Baku entries.
- **Excluded.** Zoom, titles, jets, fences, colours, HUD layout, minimap, git and pushes, and the Zandvoort banking-panel "twitching/sync" bug, which was a rendering fault.

Stage codes: GEO = track geometry, LINE = racing line, CAR = car model and calibration, ERS = energy deploy, SIM = sim/speed trace, CMP = comparison or delta.

## Reference data the pipeline already has
- **Speed traces.** Per-track CSVs from `fetch_openf1_lap.py` and `fetch_fastest_lap.py`, with columns `frame,time_s,distance,speed,throttle,brake,gear,rpm,ers_deploy`. They cover 2025 Q (13 tracks), 2026 Q/FP1/FP2 (17 files) and 2018 Q (6 tracks). The 2026 sampling rate is about 3.7–8 Hz.
- **Position X/Y/Z** (FastF1/official archive). It is map-matched to one path, so it is used only for S/F, elevation and the distance axis.
- **Lap times and sector times.** S1/S2 come from FastF1 laps and were used for the Singapore retrofit and the delta engine. 2017 pole times come from Jolpica.
- **Derived data.** `_transfer_2026.json`/`predicted_<X>.json` (2025→2026 corner ratios, lap band) and the pattern-hybrid predicted speed graph.
- **Already-built automatic checks.** `_gate_2026.py`, `_overlay_speed.py`, `_rate_vs_speed.py`, `_corner_compare.py`, `bahrain/_apex_gaps.py`, `singapore/_scan_crossings.py`, `_era_ratio_2017_2026.py`, and the LOO backtest.

## Reference data that is missing
- **ERS/deploy/SOC.** `ers_deploy` is a hard-coded 0; neither OpenF1 nor FastF1 publishes it.
- **Real lateral racing line per driver.** The position feed is map-matched.
- **Track width, edges and kerb positions.**
- **Banking/cross-slope.**
- **Lateral g and steering.** This exists only for GT3, via onboard OCR.
- **DRS.** It is not stored in the reference CSVs; Singapore pulled it ad hoc.
- **Mini-sectors finer than S1–S3.**
- **2017 and earlier telemetry.**
- **Onboard video.** Line judgements were made by the user from footage, and nothing in the pipeline ingests it.
